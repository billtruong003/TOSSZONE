## 5. `02_FPSMAP.unity.meta` GUID provenance audit

Git's rename-detection (`-M`) shows a chain:
`Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity.meta` (guid `99c9720a...`, initial commit)
→ *"copy"* to `Assets/AutoHand/Packages (Setup)/FinalIK.unitypackage.meta` (guid `1411ad34...`, commit `51ed827`)
→ *"rename"* to `Assets/_Game/Scenes/02_FPSMAP.unity.meta` (guid `2bb0648f...`, commit `5d7dc5d`, current HEAD).

**Verdict: benign git diff artifact, not a real rename or corruption.**

- The GUID **changes** at every step of the "rename" chain. A genuine Unity asset rename preserves the GUID — that's the whole point of the `.meta` file. Because the GUID changes each time, these are independent new-file events; git's similarity heuristic just paired them because generic `DefaultImporter` stub `.meta` files are ~74% identical text regardless of what they're attached to (5–7 lines of boilerplate differing only in the guid line).
- Confirmed `Assets/AutoHand/Packages (Setup)/FinalIK.unitypackage` and its `.meta` **no longer exist on disk** (removed in commit `17b3109`, see §6).
- Confirmed current `02_FPSMAP.unity.meta` guid `2bb0648f4b1edaa4ead159bdf9bcea2b` appears in **exactly one file** in `Assets/` — no collision.
- Confirmed the old `FinalIK.unitypackage.meta` guid `1411ad34cddd5154fbff788a03552579` has **zero references** anywhere in `Assets/` or `ProjectSettings/` — fully orphaned, nothing depends on it.
- Confirmed `Assets/_Game/Scenes/02_FPSMAP.unity` is a well-formed Unity scene YAML (`%YAML 1.1`, `OcclusionCullingSettings`, `RenderSettings`, etc.) — not a renamed binary package archive.

No action taken (none needed) — GUID is valid, unique, and the scene loads as a normal Unity scene asset.
